## 6. Findings — fix before re-indexing

### High: `Review` schema on case studies is self-serving

`app/case-studies/[slug]/page.tsx` emits a `Review` node whenever a study has
a testimonial, with `itemReviewed` set to Zenith Digital and the page
published by Zenith Digital. Google treats reviews about an organisation
hosted by that organisation as self-serving and **ineligible for review rich
results**. It won't earn stars, and it's the one piece of markup here that
could read as manipulative.

Currently fires on 8 of 9 case studies (all except Yacht Junky if its
testimonial is absent).

**Recommended:** delete the `reviewSchema` block and its `<JsonLd>` render.
The testimonial stays visible as conversion proof; only the markup goes. This
is the same rule already applied on `/testimonials`, so removing it makes the
site internally consistent.

### Medium: 301 redirects not verified

`CLAUDE.md` §8 lists redirects that must exist before re-indexing:

- `/webdesign` → `/services/wix-studio-website-design`
- `/marketing` → `/services/seo-aeo-ppc`
- `/branding` → nearest service
- `/contact-us` → `/contact`
- `/discovery-call` → `/book-a-call`
- `/embed-test` → drop

These are known indexed URLs on the live Wix site. They are **not** in
`next.config.ts` as far as this export can tell. Losing them means losing the
equity they carry. Verify before cutover.

### Medium: no `/contact` route exists

The redirect list points `/contact-us` at `/contact`, but there is no
`/contact` page in the app. Either build it or repoint that redirect at
`/book-a-call`.

### Low: `Article` author/publisher don't reference `ORG_ID`

Case-study pages declare a local `zenith` object for `author` and `publisher`
rather than `{ "@id": ORG_ID }`. The Service schema was corrected to use the
`@id` reference; Article still duplicates. Harmless but it fragments the
entity graph slightly.

### Low: `/faq` title is 61 characters

One character over the guideline. It will likely truncate at
`...straight answers | Zenith Dig`. Trimming to
`FAQ: pricing, process & straight answers | Zenith Digital` (56) fixes it.

### Informational: no `/pricing`, `/about`, `/blog`, `/industries` routes

`CLAUDE.md` §5 plans these. They do not exist yet, are not in the sitemap, and
nothing links to them, so there is nothing broken. Worth noting because the
homepage anchors to `/#pricing` rather than a dedicated page, which means the
pricing content can't rank on its own.
