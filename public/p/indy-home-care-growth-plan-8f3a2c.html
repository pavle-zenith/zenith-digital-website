What we need from you

1

Answer the phone.

Phone leads convert at 41%. Web form leads convert at 5%. And 42% of calls to senior care businesses go to voicemail. If a lead we paid $115 for hits your voicemail on a Saturday, no amount of ad budget fixes that. We'll ask you to commit to a 60-second answer target, 7 days a week.

2

Have caregiver capacity ready.

Five new clients need people to serve them. If you're short, we shift part of the Meta budget into recruitment - but we need to know before we start, not in November.

3

Give SEO the right clock.

The clients you get by December will come from paid ads. SEO and GEO are the month 4–12 layer - they're what makes your cost per client drop over time. We're being upfront so you're not judging the wrong channel on the wrong timeline.
