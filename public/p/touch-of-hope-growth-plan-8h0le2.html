The big play — owning “home care Indianapolis”

The whole programme comes down to one thing: being the agency a family finds and calls at the moment they decide they need help. Here's the mechanism, start to finish.

Step 1 · Reach

Ads that name the fear

Not "quality home care since 2015". Ads written around what she's actually feeling: "Mom shouldn't be alone all day." "You can't do this by yourself anymore." Copy that names the situation converts several times better than copy that lists services.

Step 2 · Land

A page built for one job

Ads point at a dedicated landing page, never the homepage. Phone number above the fold, on every scroll position, tap-to-call on mobile. Real caregiver photos, real reviews, no stock imagery. One short form for people who won't call.

Step 3 · Convert

The phone call

Phone leads convert at 41%. Web form leads convert at 5%. Everything in the funnel is built to produce a ringing phone, not a form fill - which is also why the next section matters as much as this one.

05

The GEO layer — getting cited by AI

A growing share of "who should I hire" research now happens inside ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google's AI Overviews rather than on a results page. Those systems don't rank ten blue links - they name two or three businesses. Nobody in Indianapolis home care is optimising for this yet, which is exactly why it's worth doing now.

The work is unglamorous and mostly technical: structured data that tells a machine what Touch of Hope is, where it operates and what it does; content written to answer the questions families actually ask, in a format that's easy to quote; consistent business information everywhere it appears online; and reviews, which these systems read and summarise.

The honest framing: this is the compounding layer, not the December layer. It won't deliver clients this quarter. It's what makes you the default answer in 2027 while your competitors are still buying clicks.

What we build

  • Schema markup - HomeHealthCareService, service areas, hours, credentials
  • Answer-first content - the real questions: what does home care cost in Indianapolis, when is it time, Medicare vs private pay
  • Entity consistency - name, address, phone identical across every directory that matters
  • Citation-friendly formatting - clear structure and specific claims a model can lift cleanly
  • Review sentiment - these systems read and summarise reviews, so the review work feeds this directly

07

What has to be true

1

The phone gets answered

Phone leads convert at 41%, web forms at 5%, and 42% of calls to senior care businesses go to voicemail. A lead we paid $115 for that hits voicemail on a Saturday is money burned. We'll ask for a 60-second answer target, seven days a week - it's the highest-leverage change in the whole programme and it costs nothing.

2

There are caregivers ready

Five new families need people to serve them. If you're short, we move part of the Meta budget into recruitment - but we need to know in October, not in December.

3

SEO gets the right clock

The clients you sign by December come from paid ads. SEO and GEO are the month 4–12 layer - they're what makes your cost per client fall over time. We're saying this upfront so nobody judges the wrong channel on the wrong timeline.
